Our Offerings

Clear engagement structures, defined scope, and disciplined execution.

Corinthian Academic Office operates through a small number of clearly defined engagement formats. Each offering reflects a different level of involvement, responsibility, and delegation, allowing families to choose the structure that best fits their needs.

Full Academic & Admissions Management

€5,000 per month · €25,000 per semester · €45,000 per year

This is a full-scope management engagement in which Corinthian Academic Office assumes responsibility for the student’s academic and admissions process over a defined period.

The engagement includes initial and ongoing assessment, the design of a tailored academic and admissions strategy, continuous monitoring, weekly execution sessions, and ongoing strategic updates.

We undertake complete handling of all strategic, administrative, logistical, and organizational aspects, including exams, registrations, timelines, extracurricular recommendations, documentation, and applications.

Parents are fully removed from operational involvement and receive structured weekly one-page reports, along with periodic strategic reviews.

Capacity is intentionally limited. Engagements are accepted by application only.

Academic & Admissions Strategic Review

€2,500 · One-time engagement

This is a standalone diagnostic and planning engagement designed for families seeking clarity without ongoing management.

It includes in-depth consultations with the student and family, a full assessment of the academic profile and objectives, and identification of strengths, weaknesses, and risks.

The outcome is a precise, written strategic plan detailing target universities and programs, recommended exams, timelines, priorities, concrete actions, and key risks to manage.

Execution remains the responsibility of the family unless a management engagement is subsequently agreed.

Group Programs

From €95 per week · Currently closed

Structured group programs covering admissions strategy, academic planning, and exam preparation, delivered through small-group weekly sessions.

These programs are designed to provide high-quality guidance at a more accessible price point, without the individualized management of the flagship engagement.

Group program intake is currently closed. We are not accepting further applications until further notice.
