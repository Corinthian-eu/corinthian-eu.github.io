Our Offerings

Clear engagement structures, defined scope, and disciplined execution.

Corinthian Academic Office operates through a small number of clearly defined engagement formats. Each offering reflects a different level of involvement, responsibility, and delegation, allowing families to choose the structure that best fits their needs.

Corinthian manage: Academic & Admissions Mandate

€5,000 per month · €25,000 per semester · €45,000 per year

A full-scope engagement in which Corinthian Academic Office assumes end-to-end responsibility for the student’s trajectory and admissions process for a defined period.

The mandate includes initial and ongoing assessment, design of a tailored academic and admissions strategy, continuous monitoring and execution support, and regular recalibration.

All strategic, administrative, logistical, and organizational frictions are fully removed. We handle exams, registrations, timelines, extracurricular positioning, documentation, and applications in their entirety. Parents are entirely removed from operations and receive structured weekly one-page reports.

Capacity is deliberately limited. Engagements are accepted by application only.

Corithian advice: Customized Academic & Admissions Plan

€2,500 · One-time engagement

This is a standalone diagnostic and planning engagement designed for families seeking clarity without ongoing management.

It includes in-depth consultations with the student and family, a full assessment of the academic profile and objectives, and identification of strengths, weaknesses, and risks.

The outcome is a precise, written strategic plan detailing target universities and programs, recommended exams, timelines, priorities, concrete actions, and key risks to manage.

Execution remains the responsibility of the family unless a management engagement is subsequently agreed.

Corinthian Learn: Academic & Admissions Program

From €95 per week · Currently closed

Structured small-group programs focused on admissions strategy, academic planning, and exam preparation, delivered through weekly guided sessions with a defined curriculum and milestones, teaching students everything they need to know to succeed.

The programs provide rigorous, high-quality guidance and proven frameworks at a more accessible price point, including admissions strategy; profile building tailored to target programs; guidance on extracurricular development; advice on securing strong letters of recommendation and relevant experience; advanced academic strategies to maximize performance; and structured exam preparation sessions (e.g., A-levels and the SAT). different systems per scountry, while deliberately excluding individualized management, operational handling, or bespoke admissions execution.

Group programs are designed for students who can independently execute on guidance provided, with limited personalization and no end-to-end oversight.
